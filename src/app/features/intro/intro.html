How to Play

The Goal

Select numbered buttons that add up exactly to the Goal value shown at the top of the screen.

Selecting Numbers

Tap any button to select it — it highlights in yellow. Keep tapping more buttons to build your sum. Tap a selected button again to deselect it.

The Goal counter ticks down as you select, showing how much is still needed.

What Breaks Your Combo

  • Your running sum exceeds the goal → selection cleared.
  • You tap a number whose value is already selected → selection cleared.

Scoring

Points depend on how many buttons you combined:

2 buttons10 pts
3 buttons18 pts
4 buttons28 pts
5 buttons40 pts
6 buttons54 pts

Every 20 points you earn adds 1 extra second to your timer — points accumulate across combos, so chaining big combos keeps the clock alive longer.

Levels

Each level has a score target and a countdown timer. Reach the target before time runs out to unlock the next level. There are 15 levels — level 15 is endless; beat your own high score!

Tips

  • Higher levels add more buttons and harder goals.
  • Listen for the countdown beeps — they start at 5 seconds left.
  • A new high score on level 15 goes to the global leaderboard.
