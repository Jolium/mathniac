The goal of Mathniac is to select numbers that together equal the goal value shown at the top of the screen.

Each button shows a number. Tap buttons to select them — they light up in yellow. The Goal counter decreases as you build your sum. Tap a selected button again to deselect it.

If your running total exceeds the goal, or you select a number that is already in your selection, all selected buttons clear and you must start the combo again.

Points per combo:

2 buttons10 pts
3 buttons18 pts
4 buttons28 pts
5 buttons40 pts
6 buttons54 pts

Every 20 points earned adds 1 extra second to the timer. Chain big combos to keep the clock running!

There are 15 levels, each with a score target and a countdown timer. Reach the target before time runs out to unlock the next level. Level 15 is endless — beat your own high score!

A new high score on level 15 is submitted to the global leaderboard. Good luck!
